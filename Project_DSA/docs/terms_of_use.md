# Terms of Use

Last updated: May 3, 2026

These terms describe the intended use of Don't Sign Anything during the MVP and developer preview stages.

## Educational Use Only

Don't Sign Anything is an educational document risk assistant. It summarizes documents, flags possible risk patterns, and suggests questions to ask before signing.

The app does not provide legal advice, does not replace a lawyer, and does not decide whether a document is safe to sign.

## No Attorney-Client Relationship

Using this app does not create an attorney-client relationship. For legal decisions, consult a licensed attorney.

## Rule-Based Analysis Limits

The current analysis uses rule-based detection and document classification. It can miss important language, misunderstand context, or flag clauses that may be normal in some agreements.

Always review the original document carefully.

## Sensitive Documents

Do not upload documents you are not authorized to process. Documents may contain private or confidential information.

## Saved Reports

If you sign in and save a report, the app stores the analysis report locally in SQLite. You can delete saved reports from your history.

## Mobile Developer Preview

The mobile app is currently a developer preview through Expo. It is not distributed through the Apple App Store or Google Play yet.

## Future Changes

The project is phased. Features, storage choices, integrations, and deployment behavior may change as the product matures.
